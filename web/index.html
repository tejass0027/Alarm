Why a stake works

Behavioral economists call it loss aversion — the sting of losing something you already have outweighs the pleasure of gaining the same amount. A vague sense of guilt is easy to shrug off at 6am. A stake you stand to actually lose is not. Commitment devices like this one work precisely because they make the cost of skipping concrete instead of abstract.

Questions

Is any of this real money?

No. Every balance, deposit, and stake here is a virtual number tracked locally. Nothing connects to a bank or card.

What if I can't solve the challenge?

You get as many tries as you need on each round — there's no penalty for a wrong answer, only for giving up entirely.

Can I withdraw before a 21-day streak?

No — that's the point. Withdrawals stay locked until you've solved 21 alarms in a row without giving up once.

What happens to a missed alarm's stake?

It's deducted from your balance immediately, and your streak resets to zero — the next alarm starts the count over.

Can I run more than one alarm at a time?

Yes — set as many as you like, each with its own time, stake, and repeat schedule.
