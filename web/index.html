How it works

1. Deposit

Add money to your virtual balance. Nothing real moves — it's a number you're accountable to, not a bank transfer.

2. Set the stakes

Pick a wake time, how much you're willing to lose, and whether it repeats.

3. Beat the clock

When it rings, solve a quick math or typing challenge to dismiss it. Give up, and the stake is gone.

21 days makes it a habit.

Solving your alarm builds a streak. Giving up resets it to zero. Reach a 21-day streak and you unlock the ability to withdraw — discipline earns access, not just a balance.
